History

The Rapids began as a small rafting group started by local outdoor enthusiasts. Their focus on safety, teamwork, and respect for the river shaped the company’s early identity.

Over time, The Rapids expanded with new routes, better gear, and skilled guides. Even as it grew, the company stayed committed to exciting adventures and protecting the rivers it depends on.
